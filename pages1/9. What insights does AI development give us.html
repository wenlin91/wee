and information is quietly evolving.In the past, we always thought knowledge was just 'what you memorise in your own head': at school, we competed to see who could memorise more formulas and vocabulary; once we started work, we competed to see who could immediately recall certain data or rules, as if having more stuff in your head made you better. Think carefully about what it can bring you and steadily turn it into a tool in your hands.
